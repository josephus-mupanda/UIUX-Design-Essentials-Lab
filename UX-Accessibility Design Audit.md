# Capstone Project Documentation: UI/UX & Accessibility Design Audit

---

## 1. Capstone Overview

- **Project Title:** "SwiftPay" Design Audit
- **Estimated Time:** 1 Hour

### Scenario

You are a developer on a new team at SwiftPay. A junior designer has just finished the first draft of the "Send Money" screen.

Before any code is written, your team follows a "Shift Left" principle by having developers and QA specialists review designs for potential UI, UX, and accessibility issues. Your task is to act in this role.

You will be given a read-only Figma file of the design, which contains several *intentional* flaws. Your goal is to find them, document them clearly in an "Audit Report," and explain *why* they are problems, citing the principles you learned in this module.

---

## 2. Learning Objectives

This capstone assesses your ability to:

- **Analyze** a Figma design to identify and describe violations of UI, UX, and accessibility principles.
- **Interpret** design specifications in Figma.
- **Explain** the purpose of accessibility (WCAG) and identify common violations.
- **Explain** core principles of User Experience (UX) and UI.

## 4. Learner Instructions

**Step 1 — Access the Design**
Open the "SwiftPay - Send Money" Figma file.

**Step 2 — Analyze the Design**
Carefully review the entire screen. Use Figma's inspection tools to check spacing, colors, and font sizes.

**Step 3 — Identify Issues**
Your goal is to find **at least three (3) distinct issues**. Look for problems related to:

- **Accessibility:** Is there poor color contrast? Is link text descriptive? Is there missing information for screen readers (e.g., alt-text on images)?
- **UI (User Interface):** Is spacing consistent? Is the alignment correct? Is the color palette used correctly? Is the typography (font size, weight) clear and consistent?
- **UX (User Experience):** Is the flow logical? Is it clear to the user what to do next? Are there any confusing labels or buttons?

**Step 4 — Complete the Report**
For each issue you find, fill out a row in the "Design Audit Report Template" (see below). You must:

- **Identify the issue** clearly.
- **Classify its type** (Accessibility, UI, or UX).
- **Justify your finding** by explaining *why* it is a problem, referencing a concept from the module (e.g., *"This is an accessibility issue because the light gray text on a white background fails WCAG contrast requirements, making it unreadable for users with low vision."*).

**Step 5 — Submit Your Report**
Submit your completed "Design Audit Report" for grading.
